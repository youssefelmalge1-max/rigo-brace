# Mission: Judge and direct brace trimline quality

## Why
Understand how the add-on turns anatomical landmarks into a surface-fitted trimline and then into a manufactured rim, so visual tests produce precise engineering feedback and the final brace has a continuous, Fusion-style edge treatment.

## Success looks like
- Distinguish a curve-path defect from cut-mesh faceting, fillet geometry, and shading.
- Explain the add-on's axis, front vector, angular parameter, and surface projection.
- Specify measurable acceptance criteria for one continuous trim perimeter and constant-radius fillet.
- Review front, back, side, and silhouette views without confusing smooth shading with smooth geometry.

## Constraints
- All dimensions and tolerances are expressed in millimetres.
- The trim must remain attached to the corrected patient surface and generate one manifold brace.
- Software geometry evidence never replaces orthotist review of coverage, laterality, pressure, or safety.

## Out of scope
- Choosing the patient's clinical trim prescription or corrective force system.
- Treating a visually smooth viewport as fabrication approval.

Trimline quality glossary

Use these words when reporting a defect so we change the correct layer.

TermMeaningWhat you see
Control curveThe editable cyclic Bezier path before cutting the shell.Orange line and blue control points.
Surface fittingProjection of curve points to the corrected body plus a small offset.The line follows body curvature instead of floating or intersecting.
TessellationThe triangles/edges used to approximate the surface.Faceted silhouette or irregular short triangles.
G0 continuitySegments meet at the same point only.A connected path may still have a corner.
G1 continuitySegments share a tangent direction.No visible kink at the join.
G2 continuityCurvature also changes continuously.A softer, CAD-like transition with no abrupt curvature change.
Constant-radius filletOne radius is applied along the full selected edge chain.The rounded rim has the same cross-section everywhere.
Smooth shadingInterpolated display normals; geometry is unchanged.Faces look softer, but the silhouette can remain polygonal.
Edge-band smoothingGeometry relaxation over a band next to the rim.Local bumps reduce, but radius is not guaranteed.
